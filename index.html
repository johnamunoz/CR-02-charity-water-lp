The water crisis

Nearly 1 in 10 people worldwide live without clean water.

That’s about twice the population of the United States. Most people without clean water spend hours every day walking to collect it, and in 8 out of 10 homes without water on site, that job falls to women and girls. That’s time taken away from school, work, and family.
